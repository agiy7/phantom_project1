What is PHANTOM?

PHANTOM is a free, 3-day escape room hackathon for teens in Philadelphia, run by Hack Club. You earn your invite by building projects, then meet everyone else who earned theirs in a venue full of puzzles.

How it works

Build on one project or several. There is no single right idea.

  1. Get an ideaPick any project you want to make.
  2. Start buildingPlan it, sketch it, or just start writing code.
  3. Log your hoursHackatime counts the time you spend coding.
  4. Ship itSubmit the project for review.
  5. Earn fragmentsEvery approved hour turns into fragments.
  6. Claim your inviteSpend 50 hours' worth of fragments on your invite to Philadelphia, or on shop prizes if you can't make it.

Stay, food and flights

Who is behind it?

PHANTOM is by Archer, David and Santi, and is run by Hack Club, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and network of more than 100,000 technical high schoolers. Hack Club gives teens community and grants to build projects, and has run events like Capitol, a 36-hour hackathon in Washington, D.C.
